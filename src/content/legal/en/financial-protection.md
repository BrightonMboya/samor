A safari in Tanzania takes months to plan and a real investment to make. We understand that life sometimes gets in the way: an illness, a family emergency, a flight cancelled by something outside anyone's control. When that happens, our first instinct is not to reach for a policy. It is to call you and find out what we can do.

This page explains how we protect the money you pay us, how cancellations and refunds work, what we can almost always put right, and where we work alongside partners who set their own rules.

## How your money is protected

Our guests come from all over the world, and there is no worldwide bonding agency for safari operators. So we take a practical approach to protecting you against the failure of our company:

- We pay suppliers well before you arrive, so your bookings stay in place even if our company were to fail.
- We hold all other money (our margin) in a separate bank account until after you have returned home.
- All payments go to company accounts held in Tanzania. We do not hold offshore accounts.

Everything else should be covered by your travel insurance, which we ask every guest to hold from the day they book.

## What we believe

Care is not a slogan for us. It is how we run the company. When you contact us about a change of plans, you are talking to the people who planned your trip, not a refund department. Most situations have more than one answer, and we look for the one that protects you best.

That said, nobody delivers your trip alone. Park authorities, lodges, airlines and our own team have often been paid weeks before you arrive. We are honest about what we can recover and what we cannot.

## Cancelling a safari

A safari is built around the lodges and camps where you stay. Accommodation is one of the largest parts of the cost, and almost all of it goes to properties we do not own. Each one has its own cancellation policy:

- Some partners allow free postponement to future dates. We always check this first, because it is the most common way to recover the cost of an interrupted trip.
- Most properties have cancellation charges that rise as your arrival date gets closer. The usual pattern: around 10% more than 90 days out, around 30% between 60 and 90 days, around 60% between 30 and 60 days, around 80% between 8 and 30 days, and the full charge in the final week. We negotiate with partners on your behalf, and long relationships often help.
- The most sought-after camps are usually the strictest. The more in demand a lodge is, the harder it is to release a confirmed booking.
- Internal flights are often non-refundable. Light aircraft to remote parks run on tight margins, and most carriers do not refund cancelled tickets, even with plenty of notice.
- Park fees are refundable up to one day before your safari starts.

When you cancel, we go through every line of your booking with the partners involved and work out the most we can recover. Whatever comes back, we pass on to you, minus any bank fees.

## Postponing instead of cancelling

If your dates change but your plans don't, postpone. It almost always works out better than cancelling.

We contact every partner the moment we hear from you. Many lodges allow deferral without a penalty, especially outside peak season, and we have a very good record of moving bookings without fees when we ask early. Park fees and other costs can rise over time; if they do before your new dates, we will pass that difference on.

## Travel insurance

We strongly recommend it. Travel to Tanzania involves things nobody can predict: international flight cancellations, illness or injury before you leave, the need for evacuation once you arrive. A good policy covers all of these, and can recover costs that even we cannot refund, like non-refundable accommodation or internal flights.

We provide all the documents you need to make a claim. When choosing a policy, look for trip cancellation and medical evacuation cover.

## How to reach us about a change

The fastest way is WhatsApp, or email to your planner. We aim to reply within 12 hours, often much sooner. Some answers depend on lodges and airlines getting back to us, so the most complex requests can take a little longer. Either way, your planner will stay with you until it is resolved.

There is no form to fill in and no script. You will be talking to the same people who helped you plan the trip.
