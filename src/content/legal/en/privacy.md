## Guarantee of confidentiality

At Samur Safaris, we value and respect your privacy. This policy is here so you can use our website with confidence.

## What we collect, and why

When you contact Samur Safaris through our website or on WhatsApp, we may collect personal information including your name, phone number and email address. We need it to plan your trip, send you a quotation or share an itinerary. We do not share your information with third parties, except where the law requires it or where we need to for your trip, such as passing booking details to the lodges and camps you will stay at.

We may use your email address to send you news and offers that match your interests. You can opt out at any time by replying to any message from us, or by contacting us directly.

## Data security

Protecting your personal data is a priority for us. Information is encrypted using SSL before it is sent, and our systems are protected by firewalls. We collect data about website visits to improve the site and our service, and use it for internal reporting without identifying individuals.

## Cookie usage

Our website uses cookies to improve how it works and to understand traffic. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us see how you use the site. They do not contain personal data and cannot harm your device. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

## Visitor activity monitoring

We may use analytics tools to track visitor activity, such as page visits and interactions. This helps us improve the website and our marketing. These tools collect IP addresses without identifying you personally. You can turn off cookies to prevent this tracking.

## External links

Our website links to other sites, which have their own privacy policies. Samur Safaris is not responsible for the practices of external sites, or for information you share with them.

## Questions and changes

If you have questions about how we handle your data, or want to change your consent, email us at [hello@samursafaris.com](mailto:hello@samursafaris.com).
